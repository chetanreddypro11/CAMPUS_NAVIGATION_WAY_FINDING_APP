        About CampusWay

        A wayfinding page for students and visitors of Polaris School of Technology. The campus is a fictional sample:

        Walking time assumes about 80 m per minute. Routes use the shortest walkway path and, when two paths are equal, the one with fewer steps.

        Good to know

        • Outdoor routes only. No live GPS or indoor floor plans.
        • Saved places and recent searches stay in this browser only.
        • Facilities can update every place and walkway in one file: data/places.json.
